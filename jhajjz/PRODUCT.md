# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated: a single static HTML/CSS/JS page with no build step, so the design can be reviewed as a link and ported to any stack later.

## Users

All three audiences, with equal weight (confirmed):
- Wedding families booking the couple's car, decorated cars and guest transport between venues and hotels.
- Corporate and VIP clients: delegation convoys, travel desks at 5-star hotels, airline crew pickups and drops, daily staff commutes.
- Personal hire: chauffeur-driven cars by the hour, day or month, city tours, long-term hire.

## Product Purpose

Jhajj'z rents luxury cars and coaches with chauffeurs, from a Rolls-Royce to a Volvo coach. Success is a visitor seeing the right car for their occasion and getting in touch.

## Positioning

A family-run Mohali/Chandigarh fleet running since 1998 that covers every scale of an occasion under one roof: the couple's Rolls-Royce, the vintage car for the photoshoot, the stretch limousine, the guests' Vellfires and the coaches. Chauffeur-driven only.

## Operating Context

- Showroom: SSS-106, 107, PCA Stadium Road, Sector 63, Mohali (SAS Nagar), Punjab.
- Serves Chandigarh, New Delhi, Gurgaon, Jaipur, Udaipur, Mumbai, Chennai, Kolkata, Bhubaneswar, Dehradun, Mussoorie, Rishikesh, Haridwar and Ropar.
- Bookings happen by phone and WhatsApp today.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Fleet categories: luxury cars (Rolls-Royce, Bentley, Mercedes-Benz, BMW, Jaguar), executive cabs, SUVs (Range Rover), luxury MPVs (Toyota Vellfire, Mercedes-Benz V-Class), vintage cars, convertibles, limousines (Chrysler 300C stretch), mini vans (Force Urbania), luxury coaches (Volvo, Mercedes-Benz).
- Weddings: the team handles decoration, timings and chauffeurs on the day.
- Undecided: the primary call to action (WhatsApp, call or form). The design keeps contact routes visible without committing.
- No prices, specs or opening hours are published.

## Product Principles

1. Show the cars. The fleet is the argument; every section leads with a vehicle.
2. Every occasion, every scale: one car to a convoy of coaches.
3. Real over claimed: use the real photographs and real facts (1998, the showroom, the cities); no invented proof.
4. A chauffeur is always included; never present it as self-drive.
